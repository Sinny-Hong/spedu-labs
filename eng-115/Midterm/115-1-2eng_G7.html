三、句型練習
1.他們喜歡我們。　us / like / They
→ They like us.
2.我喜歡他的哥哥。　brother / I / his / like
→ I like his brother.
3.她喜歡我的爸爸。　my / likes / She / father
→ She likes my father.
4.他們是我們的老師。　teachers / They / our / are
→ They are our teachers.
