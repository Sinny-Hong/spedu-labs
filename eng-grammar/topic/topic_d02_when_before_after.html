D02｜Before／After／When
章節目錄

《文法講義》Before／After／When

班級：______　姓名：____________

三章文法重點

章節文法重點兩種寫法
Before 之前：before 後面接較晚發生的事。I washed my hands before I ate lunch.
Before I ate lunch, I washed my hands.
After 之後：after 後面接較早發生的事。I ate lunch after I washed my hands.
After I washed my hands, I ate lunch.
When 當時：A 時段裡面發生了 B 事件。I was doing my homework when my mom came home.
When my mom came home, I was doing my homework.
閱讀提醒

英文寫在前面的事件，不一定比較早發生。先看連接詞，再判斷兩件事的關係。

第一章｜Before：在……之前

1-1 之前文法

before 後面接較晚發生的事。洗手先發生，吃午餐後發生；兩種寫法都表示同一個事件順序。

先：洗手（A） → 後：吃午餐（B）

放句中：A before B.

I washed my hands before I ate lunch.

放句首：Before B, A.

Before I ate lunch, I washed my hands.

標點提醒

連接詞放句首時，前半句後面要加逗號。

1-2 圈出先發生的事

請圈出每句中先發生的事件。粗體底線標出兩件事，圈其中一件即可。

1.
I before i .
2.
Before she , Mia .
3.
Ben before he .
4.
Before we , we .

1-3 填入事件

每組 A 先發生、B 後發生。請將提供的英文事件填入空格；每組完成兩種句型。

A. washed my hands（先）　B. ate lunch（後）　先洗手，再吃午餐。

1.
I 　washed my hands before I 　ate lunch.
2.
Before I 　ate lunch, I 　washed my hands.

A. finished her homework（先）　B. watched TV（後）　先寫完功課，再看電視。

3.
Mia 　finished her homework before she 　watched TV.
4.
Before she 　watched TV, Mia 　finished her homework.

A. brushed his teeth（先）　B. went to bed（後）　先刷牙，再睡覺。

5.
Ben 　brushed his teeth before he 　went to bed.
6.
Before he 　went to bed, Ben 　brushed his teeth.

1-4 改寫句子：句首改句中

把句首的 Before 移到句中，重新寫完整句子。事件先後不變，句中不加逗號。

1. Before I ate lunch, I washed my hands.
I washed my hands before I ate lunch.
2. Before she watched TV, Mia finished her homework.
Mia finished her homework before she watched TV.
3. Before he went to bed, Ben brushed his teeth.
Ben brushed his teeth before he went to bed.

1-5 合併句子：兩句合一

依中文提示，用 before 把兩句合成一句。請把 before 放在句中，將完整句子寫在右側。

1. I washed my hands. I ate lunch.
提示：先洗手，再吃午餐。
I washed my hands before I ate lunch.
2. Mia finished her homework. Mia watched TV.
提示：先寫完功課，再看電視。
Mia finished her homework before she watched TV.
3. Ben brushed his teeth. Ben went to bed.
提示：先刷牙，再睡覺。
Ben brushed his teeth before he went to bed.
4. We packed our bags. We left home.
提示：先整理背包，再出門。
We packed our bags before we left home.

請選出與題目事件順序相同的句子，把代號寫在括號中。

第三章｜When：當……時

3-1 當時文法

A 時段裡面發生了 B 事件。A 是當時正在進行的動作；B 是在這段時間內發生的事件。

要判斷的內容英文線索例子
A 時段：當時正在做的事was／were＋V-ingI was doing my homework.
B 事件：在那時發生的事過去式動詞My mom came home.

放句中：A 時段 when B 事件。

I was doing my homework when my mom came home.

放句首：When B 事件, A 時段。

When my mom came home, I was doing my homework.

重點提醒

兩句都表示「媽媽回家時，我正在寫功課」。When 放句首時，前半句後面要加逗號。

3-2 圈選時段與事件

請圈出每句中「當時正在進行的時段」，並在另一個粗體底線事件下寫「事件」。

1.

2.
When Dad , I .
3.
We when it .
4.
When the phone , Mom .
5.
Amy when the teacher .

3-3 填入時段與事件

依提示填入完整英文動作。先辨認時段與事件；這一節不需要自行變化動詞。

A 時段：was doing my homework　B 事件：came home

1.
I 　was doing my homework when my mom 　came home.
2.
When my mom 　came home, I 　was doing my homework.

A 時段：was reading　B 事件：called me

3.
I 　was reading when Dad 　called me.
4.
When Dad 　called me, I 　was reading.

A 時段：were playing basketball　B 事件：started to rain

5.
We 　were playing basketball when it 　started to rain.
6.
When it 　started to rain, We 　were playing basketball.

3-4 改寫句子：句首改句中

把句首的 When 移到句中，重新寫完整句子。時段與事件的意思不變；句中不加逗號。

1. When my mom came home, I was doing my homework.
I was doing my homework when my mom came home.
2. When Dad called me, I was reading.
I was reading when Dad called me.
3. When it started to rain, We were playing basketball.

3-5 合併句子：兩句合一

依中文提示，用 when 把兩句合成一句。請把 when 放在句中；已給的動詞形式不要更改。

1. I was doing my homework. My mom came home.
提示：媽媽回家時，我正在寫功課。
I was doing my homework when my mom came home.
2. I was reading. Dad called me.
提示：爸爸打電話給我時，我正在閱讀。
I was reading when Dad called me.
3. We were playing basketball. It started to rain.
提示：開始下雨時，我們正在打籃球。
We were playing basketball when it started to rain.

第四章｜綜合練習

4-1 填空題：Before／After／When

依中文提示，在空格填入 before、after 或 when。句首第一個字母要大寫。

1.

2.

3.

I was doing my homework 　when my mom came home.
4.

　Before Ben went to bed, he brushed his teeth.
5.

We went home 　after it started to rain.

爸爸打電話給我時，我正在閱讀。
