1-2 原級句型 as…as

兩個人或物具有相同程度的特徵時，使用：A + be + as + 形容詞原級 + as + B。

Amy is as tall as May.（Amy 和 May 一樣高。）

1.
Ben is as old ( / ) Tom.
2.
Amy is as ( / ) as May.
3.
This box is ( / ) big as that box.

1-3 原級句型排列

句型順序：A → be → as → 形容詞 → as → B。

1.
Ben is as tall as Tom.
2.
This box is as big as that box.
3.
Amy is as old as Ben.
4.
These books are as heavy as those books.

第二章｜比較級：比……更……

2-1 比較級基本概念與 er 型

表示兩者之間「比較……」時使用比較級。許多短形容詞直接加 er。

2-4 比較級句型 taller than

句型：A + be + 比較級 + than + B。Amy is taller than Ben.（Amy 比 Ben 高。）

小提醒｜than 表示「比……」，比較級放在 than 前面，be 動詞跟主詞配合。
1.
Ben is shorter 　than Tom.（比）
2.
Amy 　is taller than Ben.（be 動詞）
3.
These books 　are heavier than those books.（be 動詞）
4.
This flower is more beautiful 　than that flower.（比）

2-5 比較級句型操作

句型順序：A → be → 比較級 → than → B。

1.
Ben is shorter than Tom.
2.
This box is bigger than that box.
3.
Amy is happier than Ben.
4.
These books are heavier than those books.
5.
This flower is more beautiful than that flower.

3-4 最高級句型 the tallest

句型：A + be + the + 最高級 + 比較範圍。Amy is the tallest in the class.

This flower is the most beautiful in the garden.（這朵花是花園裡最漂亮的。）

小提醒｜最高級前通常加 the；in the class / in my family / in the garden 表示比較範圍。
1.
Ben is 　the shortest in the class.（冠詞）
2.
Amy 　is the tallest in her family.（be 動詞）
3.
This flower is the most beautiful 　in the garden.（在……中）
4.
These flowers 　are the most beautiful in the garden.（be 動詞）

3-5 最高級句型操作

句型順序：A → be → the → 最高級 → 比較範圍。

1.
Ben is the shortest in the class.
2.
Amy is the tallest in my family.
3.
This box is the biggest in the room.

4-4 延伸挑戰：完整句子產出

1. Amy 和 May 一樣高。
提示：Amy / as / tall / as / May
Amy is as tall as May.
2. Ben 比 Tom 矮。
提示：Ben / short / than / Tom
Ben is shorter than Tom.
3. Amy 是班上最高的。
提示：Amy / the / tall / in the class
Amy is the tallest in the class.
