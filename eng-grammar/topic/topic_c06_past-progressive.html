第一章｜認識與判讀過去進行式

1-1 什麼是過去進行式？

過去進行式用來說明過去某個時刻，某人正在做什麼。

主詞 ＋ 過去式 be 動詞（was / were）＋ V-ing

時態英文句子句子的意思
過去簡單式Amy played basketball yesterday.Amy 昨天打了籃球。
過去進行式Amy was playing basketball at five yesterday.昨天五點，Amy 正在打籃球。
現在進行式Amy is playing basketball now.Amy 現在正在打籃球。

用兩色螢光筆標記。第一色畫 be 動詞，第二色畫整個 V-ing。

Amy was tired yesterday. 裡的 was 搭配形容詞 tired，這句是過去簡單式。

1-2 看中文，判斷句子在說什麼

看句子是在說過去發生的事或狀態，還是過去某時正在做的事。從右框選時態，把代號填入括號。

（ 　 ）1.昨天五點，我打了一通電話給媽媽。
（ 　 ）2.昨晚八點，哥哥正在洗碗。
（ 　 ）3.我昨天很累。
（ 　 ）4.昨天放學時，他們正在操場跑步。
（ 　 ）5.昨天放學後，他們買了飲料。
選項

1-3 找出動詞，判斷英文時態

先標記動詞或動詞組，再從右框選出時態，把代號填入括號。

（ 　 ）1.Ben is washing the dishes now.
（ 　 ）2.Ben washed the dishes last night.

（ 　 ）4.They were tired yesterday.
（ 　 ）5.They were running in the park at seven yesterday morning.

第二章｜把 was / were 和 V-ing 接起來

2-1 看主詞，選 was 或 were

主詞過去式 be 動詞
I、he、she、it、一個人或一個事物was
you、we、they、兩個以上的人或事物were

圈出括號內正確的 be 動詞。

1.
I ( / ) eating dinner at six yesterday.
2.
You ( / ) sleeping at ten last night.
3.
The boys ( / ) playing basketball at five yesterday.

2-2 看搭配，選出 V-ing

拼法快查　play → playing　　make → making　　run → running

圈出括號內可以接在 was 或 were 後面的動詞形式。

1.
He was ( / / ) at six yesterday.
2.
We were ( / / ) a cake at three yesterday.
3.
The dog was ( / / ) in the park at seven yesterday morning.

2-3 合起來寫成過去進行式

圈出句中的 was 或 were，再圈出 V-ing。

1.
2.

2-4 填入兩個空格

第一格填 was 或 were，第二格把括號內的動詞改成 V-ing。

1.
Amy 　was 　writing (write) an email at nine last night.
2.
They 　were 　running (run) at seven yesterday morning.
3.
I 　was 　playing (play) basketball at five yesterday.

第三章｜排列、改寫與造句

3-1 先排好肯定句

先放主詞，再放過去式 be 動詞，接著說正在做什麼，最後交代時間。

誰過去式 be 動詞正在做什麼時間
Amywasreading a bookat eight last night.

把字詞排成完整句子。這組練習把時間放在句尾。

1.
They were playing basketball at five yesterday.
2.
Dad was cooking dinner at six yesterday.
3.
We were eating lunch at noon yesterday.

3-2 肯定句改否定句

在 was / were 後面加 not，原本的動作和時間照寫。

1.
Ben was watching TV at nine last night.
→Ben 　was 　not watching TV at nine last night.
2.
They were studying at seven last night.
→They 　were 　not studying at seven last night.
3.
We were playing basketball at five yesterday.
→
We were not playing basketball at five yesterday.

3-3 肯定句改疑問句

把 was / were 移到主詞前面，句尾加問號。

1.
Dad was cooking at six yesterday.
→　Was 　Dad cooking at six yesterday?
2.
They were sleeping at ten last night.
→　Were 　they sleeping at ten last night?
3.
The boys were running in the park at seven yesterday morning.
→
Were the boys running in the park at seven yesterday morning?

3-4 Yes / No 簡答

用人稱代名詞和 was / were 簡答。否定簡答可用 wasn’t / weren’t。

1.
Was Ben watching TV at nine last night?（No）
No, he wasn’t.
2.
Were the girls dancing at four yesterday?（Yes）
Yes, they were.

3-5 寫出完整的過去進行式

看人物、動作和時間，寫出一句完整的英文。

1.
爸爸（Dad）／煮晚餐（cook dinner）／昨天六點
Dad was cooking dinner at six yesterday.
2.
他們（They）／打籃球（play basketball）／昨天下午五點
They were playing basketball at five yesterday afternoon.
3.
昨天晚上八點你正在做什麼？用 I 寫一句完整的英文。
參考答案　I was watching TV at eight last night.
