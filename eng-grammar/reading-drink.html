點餐學習單 閱讀測驗 (一)

新市國中 英文科講義 班級：_______ 姓名：____________

Reading Comprehension: Ordering Drinks

Clerk: May I help you?

Tom: Yes, I want a large Black Tea with no sugar and no ice.

Mary: And I want a medium Pearl Milk Tea.

Clerk: Sure. Would you like to add anything to your pearl milk tea?

Mary: Yes, please add some pudding.

Clerk: No problem. That will be one large drink and one medium drink.

Tom: How much is the total?

Clerk: That’s $130 in total.

Multiple Choice Questions

1. What drink does Tom order?
(A) Green Tea (B) Black Tea (C) Lemon Juice (D) Coffee Latte
2. What size is Mary’s drink?
(A) Small (B) Medium (C) Large (D) Extra Large
3. What does Mary add (加料) to her drink?
(A) Pearl (B) Coconut Jelly (C) Pudding (D) Honey
4. How does Tom want his ice level?
(A) Regular Ice (B) Less Ice (C) No Ice (D) Warm
5. How much does Tom’s large Black Tea cost?
(A) $55 (B) $65 (菜單 L: $65) (C) $100 (D) $130

Reading Comprehension: Special Orders

Clerk: Hello! What would you like today?

Kevin: I’d like a medium Orange Green Tea with half sugar.

Sarah: I want a large Winter Melon Tea with less ice.

Clerk: Would you like any add-ons for the Winter Melon Tea?

Sarah: Yes, please add coconut jelly.

Kevin: Also, can I have my drink warm? It's a bit cold today.

Clerk: Of course. That will be $130 total. Here are your drinks!

Multiple Choice Questions

6. What drink does Kevin order?
(A) Green Tea (B) Fresh Milk Tea (C) Orange Green Tea (D) Honey Lemon
7. How much sugar does Kevin want in his drink?
(A) 0% (B) 50% (C) 100% (D) No Sugar
8. What is the temperature of Kevin’s drink?
(A) Hot (B) Warm (C) No Ice (D) Less Ice
9. What add-on does Sarah choose?
(A) Pearl (B) Pudding (C) Coconut Jelly (D) No add-ons
10. How much is Sarah’s large Winter Melon Tea?
(A) $55 (B) $65 (菜單 L: $65) (C) $75 (D) $130

點餐學習單 閱讀測驗 (三)

新市國中 英文科講義 班級：_______ 姓名：____________

Reading Comprehension: A Sweet Treat

Clerk: Good afternoon! What can I get for you?

Linda: I want a medium Coffee Latte, please.

Clerk: Would you like that hot or cold?

Linda: I want it hot with regular sugar.

Peter: And I would like a large Honey Lemon.

Clerk: Sure. Would you like to add anything to the Honey Lemon?

Peter: Yes, please add some pearls and coconut jelly.

Clerk: Okay! That will be $140 in total.

Multiple Choice Questions

11. What drink does Linda order?
(A) Black Tea (B) Fresh Milk Tea (C) Coffee Latte (D) Ice Cream Black Tea
12. How much sugar does Linda want in her coffee?
(A) No Sugar (B) Half Sugar (C) Regular Sugar (100% 全糖) (D) 0% Sugar
13. What is the temperature of Linda’s drink?
(A) Hot (B) Warm (C) No Ice (D) Room Temperature
14. Which two add-ons does Peter want?
(A) Pearl and Pudding (B) Pudding and Coconut Jelly (C) Pearl and Coconut Jelly (D) No add-ons
15. How much is a large Honey Lemon (without add-ons)?
(A) $55 (B) $65 (菜單 L: $65) (C) $130 (D) $140
